# Notes from the rendering atelier

Margo Field Notes is a tiny demonstration publication: semantic Markdown, a host-owned visual system, and images delivered in formats browsers actually use.

## Latest story

### [Inside the Margo rendering atelier](field-notes.html)

One render becomes an article, a responsive hero, and a format study without smuggling public-web policy into the core HTML API.

![A top-down editorial image-format study in dusty pink, indigo, cream, and teal.](assets/format-study.png)

## What this example proves

- The landing page uses the generic `RenderHTMLPage` contract.
- The article opts into canonical and social metadata through `webpublication`.
- AVIF, WebP, JPEG, PNG, and GIF assets are copied and loaded as real image bytes.
- The same page stays readable when JavaScript is absent.
